Diwali 2026: follow the money. ₹60,000 crore of festive ad spend, read through what I'm learning in Advertising & Sales Promotion at XLRI.
Diwali falls on 8 November, about three weeks later than last year. Brands got a longer runway, and they're using it.
The pot grew 13-17%. Festive ad spend is forecast at ₹60,000-62,000 crore, up from about ₹53,000 crore in 2025.
Two of every three rupees: digital. Digital is forecast at 67.8% of festive spend. It lets brands show up at the moment of choice, not just in memory.
7,200 brands, one feed. Creator spend is heading for ₹900 crore, up 29%. Creators work because people copy who they trust. Seven thousand brands in one week is noise.
Quick commerce: up 50%. ₹2,200 crore this festive season. That's purchase facilitation, the fifth communication effect: the ad sits one tap from the cart.
Billboards: sold out. Outdoor expects 15-20% growth, and premium sites are effectively fully booked. Media is bought months ahead. Brands that planned early own the best walls.
Budgets on a short leash. Brands aren't locking full festive budgets months in advance. They want proof first, the same shift agency pay made from commission to performance.
Diwali doesn't build brands. It cashes them in. Binet & Field: roughly 60% brand building, 40% activation. Festive spend is mostly the 40%. It runs on memory built the other 11 months.
