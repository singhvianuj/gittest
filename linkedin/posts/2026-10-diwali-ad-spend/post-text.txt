₹60,000 crore. That's what brands are expected to spend on festive advertising in India this year, up from roughly ₹53,000 crore in 2025.

I've spent the last few weeks in Advertising & Sales Promotion at XLRI, so I read this year's Diwali numbers through what we've covered in class.

The money isn't spread evenly. Two of every three rupees go to digital. Quick commerce ad spend is up 50%. 7,200 brands are paying creators. Premium billboards were booked out weeks ago.

My read: most of that spend is activation. It works at the moment someone is choosing, one tap away from the cart.

And activation can only cash in memory a brand has already built. The other 11 months of the year did that work.

So the brands that win this Diwali mostly won it in May.
