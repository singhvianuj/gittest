Five global brands came into India this year. No two of them came in the same way.

Off-White licensed its name to an Indian retailer. Lululemon signed a franchise with Tata CLiQ. SKIMS handed the whole operation to Reliance Brands. UNIQLO and IKEA own their Indian businesses outright.

In Global Marketing at XLRI, the slide on entry modes ends with one line: each option carries a different degree of risk and a different degree of control.

Watching it play out in one market, in one year, made that line real for me.

My read: the entry mode is a brand decision before it's a legal one. If the product is the brand, a partner can sell it. If the store is the brand, you'll want to run the store yourself.

And Dunkin' is the reminder of the other side. A franchise can also end on the partner's terms. That's happening on 31 December.
